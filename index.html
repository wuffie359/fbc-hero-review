Homepage Hero Prototypes
for First Baptist Church Huntersville

9 prototypes across 3 strategic directions Live site: mishtoba.wixstudio.com/fbc-h Updated today

These are conversation-starters, not finished work. The goal is to give leadership concrete options to react to — and find the direction that feels right for our church.

Every prototype here uses the same masthead as our live Wix site — same logo, same nav (About / Sermons / Ministry / Outreach / Giving), same dropdowns, same teal-on-white styling. What changes is the hero below.

The nine prototypes explore three different strategic directions. None of them are finished. None of them are right or wrong. They’re reactions worth comparing — and the best feedback often sounds like “I like the messaging from row 2 with the photo style of row 3.”

01
Newcomer-First Strategy

The Concierge Approach

Optimized for the first-time visitor deciding whether to come on Sunday. The hero answers their unspoken questions: when is it, where is it, what should I expect, can my kids come, what do I wear, will I be singled out? A single confident invitation: “Plan Your Visit.” Trades brand-establishing tone for conversion clarity.

A few things worth knowing

Photos are placeholders

Several prototypes use stock or unrelated imagery as placeholders. Each variant includes a "Photo direction" overlay describing exactly what to commission. None of these are the final photos.

Masthead matches the live site

Every prototype uses the actual FBC Huntersville logo and the live Wix nav structure (About / Sermons / Ministry / Outreach / Giving) with dropdowns matching what’s currently published. The hero is what changes.

People imagery, intentionally anonymous

Direction 03 features people without identifying any individual: backlit silhouettes, congregation seen from behind, close-up crops on hands. A two-hour photo session on a Sunday could capture all four mosaic crops authentically.

Mixing and matching is encouraged

“I like the headline treatment from Direction 02 with the photo style from Direction 03” is exactly the kind of feedback that moves us forward. The prototypes are deliberately separate so we can see the strategies clearly.

Questions to bring to leadership

  • Who’s the hero for? A first-time visitor, a long-time member, or both? The answer changes which direction wins.
  • What does our photography look like? Are we comfortable featuring our people on the homepage even anonymously, or do we want to lead with place/atmosphere?
  • How direct should we be? Do we want to tell visitors when, where, what to expect — or trust them to find that on their own?
  • Refine or reimagine? Do we like the existing messaging and want a stronger visual treatment of it, or are we open to changing what the hero says entirely?
  • What’s the right CTA? Watch a sermon? Plan a visit? Both? Neither — just an invitation?
  • Where do we want to invest in production photography? A single hero photo, multiple close-ups, or an ongoing library?
